Point `docsRss`, `docsAtom`, and `docsJsonFeed` at any `content()` source whose documents carry a `date` (frontmatter or `YYYY-MM-DD-` filename prefix).

```ts
import { docsRss, docsAtom, docsJsonFeed, listPosts } from 'acrolls/docs';
```

Newest posts sort first. Drafts and hidden entries stay out of the feeds.
