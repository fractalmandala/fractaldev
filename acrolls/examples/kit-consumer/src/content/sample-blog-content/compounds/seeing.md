The work is not done when the file is written. The work is done when I have seen it, in a window, doing the thing, without a dummy behind the click.

## The photo is the assignment

[Look at it](../molecules/look-at-it.md). The eval screenshot showed the blowout. That is first. Not the steps after. Dual view: the thing, and the thing as it is running. HMR is a feeling. The browser is in the app. Use it. I am in Helium. You keep opening a pane I am not in. Hi, are you there.

## Empty is visible

[Empty is not shipped](../molecules/empty-is-not-shipped.md) because empty is something you can see if you click. Stubs survive in files. They die in windows. I want you to experience the frustration. Forty percent placeholder is a number I got by looking, not by believing a registry.

Grade A includes the absence of theatre. Minimum, perfectly working. Not a mock area that spills. Not extra green. Not "already traced" when the tracing has not begun.

## The priest

The silent priest [watches](../ai/brahman-watcher.md). The [hollow oracle](../ai/hollow-oracle.md) can speak with the lights off. Understanding-like outputs are not understanding. I do not need the agent to be conscious. I need it not to skip the seeing. Pay the watcher. Do not pay the press release.

This is why [let me confirm](../ai/let-me-confirm.md) is not fussiness. Until I have seen it, it has not worked. I am the yajamana. The fruit of an unseen act does not accrue. It just sits in a folder, looking shipped.
