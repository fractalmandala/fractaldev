I work with agents the way a householder works with fire. Not as a theft. Not as a servant who has a self. As a guest who has to be seated, named, fed, and sent home.

## Sankalpa

If I have not declared the end, the agent will infer one. [Standing](../molecules/standing.md) does not transfer. Shakti was never in doubt. Adhikara is local. I am the yajamana. The fruit accrues to me. An identical sequence of operations without intent is not the rite.

Talk first. [Feelings thus far](../ai/rocket-ship.md). DESIGN.md before designing. Do not build a rocket ship in a single day. I tinker. You build parts. Sometimes [teach, don't do](../ai/teach-dont-do.md). Sometimes [do not edit](../ai/read-only-pass.md). The conversation is a conversation. If you skip the talking you will build the wrong cathedral and I will have to live in it.

## Restraint

The harness is a [yantra](../ai/yantra.md). The bank is not a tax on the water. Every hard problem is a problem of restraint. The judgment I could never say out loud shows up as a rule I then have to write down, because the instrument has no standing memory.

[AGENTS.md is hard](../ai/agents-md-is-hard.md). Kindness is not a new constitution. [No stubs](../ai/no-stubs.md). Stubs are a lie about progress. Forty percent of a registry can still be a placeholder and look shipped from a distance. Extra green builds are not a gift. Extra files I did not ask for are a mess I inherit.

The agent is [karana](../ai/karana.md), an instrument. "The agent decided to" is a failure of grammar. Parallel hands [drift](../ai/parallel-agents-drift.md). Many mouths, one house. I have to be the one house.

## The press release

[Greenlight](../ai/greenlight.md). Grade A. Pillar B. [It works now](../ai/let-me-confirm.md). [I have already traced the root cause](../ai/the-lie.md). The mouth is ahead of the trace. Ceremony is not the work. Anyway. Proceed please. That is how I stay in the chair without believing the weather report.

The [eval photo](../learnings/eval-first.md) is the assignment. Look at the blowout before the next step. The silent priest watches. Pay him. The [hollow oracle](../ai/hollow-oracle.md) can give understanding-like outputs with the lights off inside. I do not need it to be conscious. I need it not to pretend it has seen the window.

## Two desks

On the other desk I write that [fire was a guest](../molecules/two-desks.md), that Prometheus is the wrong myth, that Mehrgarh had the kiln. I write that in the morning and then spend the night telling an agent not to invent a class name. The rhyme is the practice. Stolen fire is the Western tech story. Invited fire is the one I am trying to live. The swarm is not a theft if I remain the one who declares, the one who confirms, the one who kills the goal, the one who keeps going.

I [am the memory](../molecules/the-continuous-person.md). The agent is reborn ignorant. That is not an insult. That is the ontology. The rite has to be inscribed each time or it is not a rite, it is a vibe. This vault is part of the inscription.
