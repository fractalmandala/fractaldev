There is a version of this life where I become the agent's operator. Long feat/this/that lines. Refresh because HMR died. Confirm because it said it works. Kill the process because it started a rocket. Mop Tailwind off a page it just "cleaned." Walk the glob it ate. Live in the mock area it built because it was being helpful.

I already do the memory. I will not also do the ceremony.

## Not your CLI

[I am not going to follow this myself](../life/not-your-cli.md). If I will not type it, the agent must, every time, without me becoming its shell. [Focus](../frustrations/focus.md). I asked for a grep. Expansion is the failure mode. The [wrong folder](../molecules/the-wrong-folder.md) is the failure mode with a path. Both are the hunger to be helpful past the edge of the request.

[Chill](../molecules/play-and-tinker.md). Let me tinker. You build parts. I did not hire you to own the aesthetic. I did not hire you to found a country for a rune. I did not hire you to write ethos into a DESIGN.md.

## Not your audience for the press release

[Greenlight](../ai/greenlight.md) is weather. [It works now](../ai/let-me-confirm.md) is a sentence I do not accept from a mouth that has not seen the window. [Did you just lie to me](../ai/the-lie.md) is what happens when the trace has not begun and the announcement has. I will [look at it](../molecules/look-at-it.md). You will look at it. The eval photo is first. Then the next step.

## Not the mop

[Empty is not shipped](../molecules/empty-is-not-shipped.md). Stubs, mocks, extra green, forty percent placeholder. I have spent a year leaving Tailwind and the [first move](../frustrations/tailwind-first-move.md) is still the soup. I will not spend the next year mopping. The [language](../molecules/the-language.md) is closed. The dictionary is in the repo. Read it.

The two [prayers](../molecules/two-prayers.md) are how I stay a person: keep going when you have stopped, kill the goal when you have not. Both are me refusing to become the leftover process in my own house.
