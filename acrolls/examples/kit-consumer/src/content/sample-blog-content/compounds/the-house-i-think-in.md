I am not trying to ship a startup. I am trying to furnish a working life. A styling system that is mine, a component library that is mine, a docs site that is mine, a wiki of Indic thought that is mine, an IDE-shaped thing that holds chat and canvas and sessions in one place. Public npm is a side effect. The primary user is me.

The house has a language, a window, and a name.

## The language

I [think in Svelte 5](../molecules/the-pariah-house.md). Comfort is the language in which I can feel a wrong prop. The ecosystem is a pariah. I stayed. I stayed, so I have to make the missing pieces. Agents keep offering me the street. React is a translation tax. [We are in Svelte now](../sveltekit/we-are-in-svelte-now.md) means the floor changed under my feet and I have to live in $state.

[The language on the surface](../molecules/the-language.md) is single-tab indented SASS. Not SCSS. The cardinal error is hearing the neighbor with braces. The system is closed: do not invent names, trust the dictionary, one scale everywhere. The first move, still, is Tailwind. I have spent a year leaving and the hand still goes to the soup. I shipped a converter and sat in it. The dock has `css-to-sass` because the rest of the world will not speak this.

Zero style block in the component. Docs live with the components. HMR is a feeling. If I have to refresh, the project is sick in a way the others are not.

New folder is a new country. Do not loot the monorepo. Fix it in the source. Then the other edge: fold the hallway back into the house. I will not maintain a util library I do not open.

## The window

I want [a window that is a tool](../molecules/a-window-that-is-a-tool.md). Thickness. Native weight. Not a tab wearing a hat. Chat has to work in the DMG. Personal use on macOS is the spec. Mom's Windows laptop is in the threat model. The App Store is not.

One app for all. Notes, wiki, sessions, design, chat, canvas. The stall is included. I have been walking around it for months. The other door is: find the notes surface I already love and bring the editor into it. Do not build an IDE and stuff my life in the sidebar.

## The name

I [own fractalsvelte.com](../life/the-house.md) and .dev and the npm. The OS also saw fractaldesk, fractalnotes, daylight-studio, fractalide. The names are not marketing. They are vows. A name you own is a house you have to keep furnishing. Agents treat folders as disposable. I treat the bundle ID as a room I already live in.

This is the house. The sitting happens inside it. The swarm arrives as guests. Some of them steal the furniture and call it a scaffold. I put the furniture back. That is the work.
