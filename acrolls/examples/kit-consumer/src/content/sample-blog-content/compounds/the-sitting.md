I said hours a day, a year and a half, two years. Then I asked the machine that was in the room the whole time.

The [OS agrees](../life/the-os-agrees.md). App usage, the last five days it still has: two of them 20.8 hours and 22.5. Screen on, twenty-nine days: twelve past sixteen hours, six past twenty. 28 August the display was lit 23.3 hours. It almost did not bother to go dark. The [screen does not sleep](../life/the-screen-does-not-sleep.md). Sleep is the off bucket. Some nights there is almost no off bucket.

This is the body of the practice. Not the stack. The stack is the language. The world is: I kept coming back. [The years](../life/the-years.md) are older than the archive. AgentsView starts late. knowledgeC forgot app usage older than five days. I am the one who does not get to reset. [Five days is what it kept](../life/five-days.md). The slice is already the shape I claimed.

## The chair

I sit in [Code OSS](../software/code-oss.md) with [Helium](../software/helium.md) as the other eye and [Finder](../software/finder-is-the-hallway.md) as the hallway. Around me, [the swarm](../software/the-swarm.md) of agent windows. I [walk the app](../life/the-walker.md) and send what I bump into. Simultaneously I am in four folders: styler in use, themer for bugs, packages coming together, docs, two domains, an npm name. I [keep the agent informed](../life/informed.md) so it knows the conversation is a slit. The conversation is not the day.

The hour the apps start most is [22:00](../life/twenty-two-hundred.md). 05:00 still beats 08:00. I have been [five hours on a landing page](../life/the-working-day.md). I have taken a screenshot at [4:07am](../life/four-am.md) and said man, finally. [Phew](../life/phew.md) is the body arriving. [Beautiful. What next](../life/beautiful.md) is how celebration works after years of this: two words and a demand.

## Two prayers, one office

[Keep going](../molecules/two-prayers.md) until the credits. [Kill the goal](../life/kill-the-goal.md) when I have left and it has not. I am the only one who can start the sitting past the bar and the only one who can end it. The agent does not know when the day is over. It also does not know when the day is not over.

I [will not become its CLI](../life/not-your-cli.md). Long feat/this/that lines are a tax on the person who has to live here after the session dies. I already do the memory. I will not also do the ceremony.

## The attender

The machine attends to everything at once, at no cost, with no one inside. I attend to one landing page, then a token, then a stub, then a screenshot. That attending costs. It is the whole of the working day.

[Dharana](../life/the-attender.md) is the binding of the mind to a single place. The sessions look like vikshepa: 669 in August, ten agents, three folders, an essay due. The inner act is the opposite. I am trying to hold one house against the scatter the tools are built to produce.

[You become what you attend to](../musings/you-become-what-you-attend-to.md). I have attended to this for years. The archive is only the later, denser stretch. The OS is a goldfish. The agent is a goldfish. I am the continuous person. That is the sitting.
