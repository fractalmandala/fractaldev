There is a garden and there is an inner ring. Flattening them into "my blog" is how you lose the person.

## The garden

2009's 42. Is it a simulation. 2014's fight over a letter in whisky. 2023, learning SvelteKit with ChatGPT, auth notes, hovers, "I haven't taken a liking to styling libraries." McKenna. Unpoems. Give rope I climb. A dharmapunk 3023. A novel that shipped. A prompted AIT polemic that should live in a drawer, not on a throne.

This is the prakara. Dates visible. WIP marked. The 2009 question next to the 2026 sleeper so you can see the person getting slower and more dangerous.

## The inner ring

The later essays are a different instrument. Same attender, slower register, a concrete fact in the first breath, spaced hyphens, IAST when the load-bearing word is Sanskrit. The [rotation](../essays/daily-rotation.md): decolonization, dharma, consciousness, deep history, technology as harness. Do not walk that room alphabetically. Walk it as [the garden and the inner ring](../molecules/the-garden-and-the-inner-ring.md) already say: one claim on the door.

Fire was a guest. Someone is looking. Standing does not transfer. History is the wrong word. The categories were imported. Other worlds I tried on. The table, last, door open.

## One stream

[Dharma is not a side quest](../molecules/dharma-is-not-a-side-quest.md). The wiki of Indic thought sits next to the hover in SvelteKit because I am one skull. [Two desks](../molecules/two-desks.md). The weekday essay on Agni, the night session on inline styles. The rhyme is the practice.

If you lead with SvelteKit tutorials you get a docs site. If you hide the whiskey you get a think-tank. If you alphabetize the later essays you get a filing cabinet. The house is rings. The attender is one. The instruments are two. That is enough.
